FAQ

Got Questions?

What's your typical process for a new project?

+

We start with a discovery phase to understand your goals, audience, and competitors. From there, we move into strategy, concept development, production, and post — keeping you in the loop at every stage.

How long does a project usually take?

+

We move fast — as fast as you need. Timing is scoped to your specific needs. Our services flex whether you're producing a single hero piece or building a repeatable content system.

Do you offer packages or custom quotes?

+

Both. We have starter packages to keep things simple, but also offer custom quotes for projects with unique needs. Tell us what you're planning — we'll make it happen.

Can you work with our existing marketing team?

+

Absolutely. We seamlessly integrate and collaborate with your team so you feel confident sharing your brand's vision and goals. From idea to execution to delivery — we support marketing teams at every stage.

What sets DUAL apart from other production companies?

+

The duality in our name is our superpower. We balance the strategic left brain with the creative right brain. We do both high-end commercial work and nimble social content. Plus, we have our own 5,000 sq ft studio, FPV drone capability, and an in-house post team.
